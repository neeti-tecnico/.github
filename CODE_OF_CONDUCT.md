# Code of Conduct

NEETI projects are open to every student, whatever their experience level. To keep it that way:

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not people. No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- **Be welcoming to beginners.** Everyone started somewhere. Explain instead of mocking.
- **Be constructive.** In reviews, suggest how to improve, not just what's wrong.
- **Respect privacy.** Don't share other people's personal data, in code or anywhere else.

## Reporting

If something makes you uncomfortable, contact the NEETI board (direção) at neeti@tecnico.ulisboa.pt <!-- replace with the real address -->.
Reports are handled confidentially. People who break these rules may be removed from the organization.
